Today's check uses the midpoint of the developer's listed price per m² against the 2026 median of ready resales in the same DLD area and unit type. Indicative: a different product from the area's stock (branded, waterfront) reads as a premium. Villas compare built-up price per m² and are noisier.

5

The launch price against the area's ready stock predicts more than the brand.

Apartments launched 2022–2024, measured against other resales of the same years: launches priced below the area's ready median outperformed Dubai by 5.4 points and 8% of them resold below launch; launches 60–100% above underperformed by 2.1 points with 15% below launch; 100%+ above underperformed by 3.9 points with 26% below launch (1,769 project cells with 10+ resales). Screen every launch on this before the brochure.

6

Half of a developer's past outperformance carries into its next projects.

Splitting each developer's resales into projects launched before and after 2019 (35 developers with 50+ pairs on both sides), the rank correlation is 0.46 and the slope 0.55. Bottom-quartile developers stayed at the bottom 67% of the time; top-quartile ones stayed top 44% of the time and never fell to the bottom. The record is a better filter against chronic laggards than a way to pick the next winner, and the premium charged for a famous name is where the edge gets priced away.

7

The payment plan is the off-plan trade, in both directions.

8

Rents stalled in 2026 while prices kept rising; yields compressed.

9

Buying at launch still pays the best yield on cost, if the launch price is right.

Method and sources

How the numbers are built

Built by Oltremare on Dubai Land Department open data and Ejari rent contracts. Every figure is reproducible from the registers.

Base
IRR, all paid
Cash out at purchase: the developer price of the matched floor plan plus 4% DLD registration and AED 3,000 of fees. Rent from handover (or purchase, when bought ready) to resale at the Ejari median new-contract rent of the project and unit type in each year, 75% kept for apartments and 85% for villas. Cash in at resale: the registered price less AED 3,000. Solved monthly; capped at the 1st and 99th percentiles.
Capital IRR
The same cash flows without rent. Area and project figures on this page use the capital IRR computed by Oltremare on the pairs; developer and unit-type figures use the full benchmark IRR with rent.
vs Dubai
The pair's IRR minus other developers' pairs of the same unit type bought and resold in the same years; "stage-matched" also matches on whether the resale came before or after handover.
Yields
Median new-contract rent of a year divided by the median ready resale price of the same unit type in the same DLD area and year, gross, per square metre for apartments. Cells need 20 contracts and 20 resales.
Payment plan
A neutral linear schedule, equal monthly instalments from purchase to planned completion; a unit flipped before the plan ends returns the resale price less the unpaid balance.
Live catalogue
Projects on sale, prices, unit counts, payment plans and handover dates come from the Alnair Client API under Oltremare's agency account; the launch check and the developer record are Oltremare's, computed from the DLD tables above.
What is left out
Tax, financing, furnishing, the value of own use, and service charges beyond the uniform net-rent share. Premium communities with 30–40% service charges are flattered; JVC or Dubailand at 15–20% are penalised.
Small samples
Figures with few resales are noisy: short holds give extreme annualised rates. The count sits beside every number; the explorer hides cells under the threshold you choose.
Survivorship
Only units that were resold are measured. Buyers who still hold are not in the record, and nor are projects without resales.
